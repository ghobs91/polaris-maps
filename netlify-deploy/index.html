Everything you expect from a map app — minus the middleman

Polaris looks and feels like the navigation app you already know. Underneath, the data comes from open projects and the people around you.

Directions that keep up

Voice-guided turns, off-route detection, and automatic rerouting. ETAs adjust as traffic changes — and a faster route is suggested before you commit to the jam.

Live traffic from the road

Colour-coded congestion fused from open feeds and anonymous speed probes shared by nearby devices — plus accidents, closures and hazards you can report yourself.

Maps that outlive the signal

Download a region once — tiles, places, routing graphs and search indexes — and keep navigating on a plane, a trail, or a dead-zone highway.

Search that answers instantly

Local indexes answer first, then open data fills in the rest. Find coffee shops open now, or a place you half-remember by name, category, or what's nearby.

Every mode, one trip

Bus, train, subway and ferry in a single itinerary, with live departures, route lines on the map, and park-and-ride plans that mix driving with transit.

Look before you go

Street-level photos from Panoramax, Mapillary and Street View, or capture your own as you travel — geotagged, privacy-blurred and signed on your device.

No servers in the middle

Most map apps route everything through a data centre. Polaris treats every phone as a node in an encrypted mesh, so map data travels between the people who need it.

  1. 1

    Your device is the node

    A keypair generated on your phone is your identity. Contributions are signed locally, so nothing leaves your device unsigned or unattributed to you.

  2. 2

    Data finds its way to you

    Peers discover each other over a distributed hash table, then exchange signed traffic reports, region packs and map data over encrypted connections.

  3. 3

    It gets better as it grows

    Regions you download are re-seeded to neighbours, and the map you edit is the map everyone sees. Add a device, add capacity — not another bill.

How the P2P layer works

Nothing phones home, because there’s no “home”

There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in Polaris, and no account to create. Your traffic, imagery and place contributions are yours to switch off at any time in Settings → Privacy.

No ad or analytics SDKs

Nothing in the app exists to profile you or sell your attention.

Anonymous by design

Traffic probes are speed and location only — signed, rotated, never tied to you.

You control the tap

Traffic telemetry, imagery and place contributions are opt-out, any time.
