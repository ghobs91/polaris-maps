Polaris Maps is a navigation app that puts you in control. Get turn-by-turn
directions, live traffic, and offline maps — with no ads, no tracking, and no
account required.

WHY PEOPLE USE POLARIS
• Clear, calm navigation that just works
• Live traffic from nearby drivers and open data
• Download maps and keep navigating when you lose signal
• Search places, plan transit trips, and explore street-level photos
• Your privacy choices are yours — change them anytime

GET WHERE YOU'RE GOING
• Turn-by-turn voice directions
• Automatic rerouting when traffic builds up
• ETAs that update as conditions change
• Suggestions that combine driving and transit

SEE WHAT'S AHEAD
• Color-coded traffic at a glance
• Warnings for accidents, closures, hazards, and construction
• Report incidents to help other drivers
• Satellite, terrain, and street-level views

FIND ANYTHING
• Search by name, category, or what's nearby
• See hours, phone numbers, photos, and reviews
• Save favorites and build lists of places
• Import your saved places from Google Maps

TRAVEL YOUR WAY
• Plan trips across bus, train, subway, and ferry where available
• See route lines and stop departures
• Download a region for offline search and routing
• Use CarPlay on supported vehicles

OPEN MAPS, HONEST PRIVACY
Polaris is built on OpenStreetMap and other open data. There are no advertising
or analytics SDKs, and we don't sell your data.

Location powers navigation. Traffic telemetry shares anonymous speed data with
nearby devices to build live traffic; it's on by default and can be turned off
in Settings → Privacy. Street-level imagery and place contributions are also on
by default during setup and can be switched off at any time.
