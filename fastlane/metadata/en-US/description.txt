Polaris Maps is a peer-to-peer navigation app built entirely on open map data.
Turn-by-turn directions, live traffic, place search, public transit, offline
maps, and community street-level imagery — without routing your every move
through a single company's servers.

WHY POLARIS
Many navigation apps send the world's location data to one company's cloud.
Polaris flips that model: your device can contribute anonymous traffic data
directly to nearby devices over an encrypted peer-to-peer mesh. No ads. No
tracking. No account required.

NAVIGATION
• Turn-by-turn guidance with GPS snap-to-route and off-route detection
• Automatic rerouting when traffic builds up ahead
• Congestion- and traffic-aware ETAs

LIVE TRAFFIC
• Colour-coded traffic built from crowd-sourced speed probes shared between
  devices, blended with open traffic feeds
• Optional commercial feed only used to seed areas with no peer data
• Contribute anonymous probes with a rotating ID — opt in, switch off any time

INCIDENTS
• Report accidents, road closures, hazards, construction and more
• Reports are cryptographically signed and shared peer-to-peer
• Warnings appear ahead while you navigate

SEARCH, TRANSIT & OFFLINE
• Fast place search with open data and rich place details
• Public transit trip planning and stop departures where available
• Download offline region packs so maps keep working without a signal
• Community-captured street-level imagery

BUILT ON OPEN DATA
Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Places from Overture Maps.
No proprietary map lock-in.

PRIVACY FIRST
Traffic telemetry is optional and can be disabled at any time. We run no
advertising or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data. Read the full
policy at https://polarismaps.app/privacy
